Six levels, one drawer.

Six direct power levels span 85–180°F across four heating methods: preheating cups, plates, and cookware; slow, low-temperature cooking; proofing and drying; and holding finished dishes warm without overcooking them.

Seal in what matters.

Three adjustable vacuum levels seal jars, marinate in minutes, and preserve fruit, jams, and even an opened bottle of wine. Paired with a combi-steam oven, it unlocks precise sous-vide preparation.
