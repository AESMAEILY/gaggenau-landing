01 — Heritage

Forged in the Black Forest.

Since 1683, Gaggenau has shaped metal into instruments of precision. What began as an iron forge in a small German valley became the origin of the modern professional kitchen.

02 — The Interior

Precision, from the inside out.

Open the door and the engineering shows itself: a rear fan, a full-width rack, and a cavity built for even, exacting heat on every level.

03 — The Door

Hand-finished, by design.

Multi-layer glazing and a hand-polished stainless door hold heat within a fraction of a degree — cool to the touch, precise within.

04 — Detail

Anodized aluminum. Zero compromise.

The dial and trim are milled and anodized by hand, weighted for a tactile response found nowhere else in the kitchen.

05 — Intelligence

Precision you can see.

A full-color display and dial control 4D Hot Air technology — circulating heat from every direction for exact, repeatable results.

06 — The Instrument

An instrument, not an appliance.

Three centuries of metalwork, distilled into one machine. This is what happens when engineering is treated as craft.

The Collection

One design language, every appliance.

Oven, coffee machine, and warming drawer share a single visual signature — the same handle-free glass, the same anodized dial — so a wall of appliances reads as one considered composition.

The Interface

Every gesture, considered.

The anodized dial doubles as a display bezel — turn to set temperature, press to confirm. A full-color screen guides every step, from preheat to done, without a single button in sight.

Preservation

Seal in what matters.

A dedicated vacuuming drawer seals jars, marinates in minutes, and extends freshness — precision engineering applied well beyond the oven itself.

Finish

Graphite or stainless. Your standard.

Every appliance is available in a graphite or stainless steel finish, so the same instrument can disappear into dark cabinetry or stand out against a brighter, minimalist room.

Warming Drawers

Held at exactly the right temperature.

Plates, cups, proofing dough, or a finished dish waiting on the table — the warming drawer holds each at the precise, gentle heat that keeps it ready, not cooking.

Coffee Machines

Espresso, finished in the same standard.

The fully automatic machine grinds and brews directly into the cup, built into the same glass, steel, and anodized dial as the oven beside it — one appliance wall, one language.

Precision Timing

Set it, and walk away.

Program a cook time down to the minute. The display counts down, the dial glows to mark it, and the oven carries the rest — precise enough to trust without checking.
